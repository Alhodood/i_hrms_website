Recruitment

Recruitment in ERP systems helps organizations manage the entire hiring cycle from job posting to onboarding in a centralized, efficient way. It allows HR teams to create and publish job openings across multiple platforms directly from the ERP. Features like applicant tracking enable easy management of candidate applications, resumes, and communication history. Interview scheduling tools streamline coordination between candidates and interviewers, while evaluation forms standardize candidate assessments. Integration with HR modules allows automatic creation of employee profiles once a candidate is hired, ensuring a smooth onboarding process. Advanced reporting and analytics provide insights into hiring trends, recruitment efficiency, and talent acquisition performance.

Job Posting Management & Tracking

The ERP enables HR teams to create job vacancies with comprehensive details such as job title, responsibilities, qualifications, location, salary range, and benefits. Job postings can be published directly to the company career page, job portals, and social media platforms, ensuring maximum visibility. The system allows HR to track applications and monitor the status of each job in real time. This centralized approach ensures consistent branding and reduces manual follow-ups. Managers can quickly analyze which postings attract the best candidates, improving recruitment effectiveness.

Applicant Tracking System (ATS)

All incoming applications are organized automatically in a centralized dashboard for easy access. Recruiters can track candidates through each hiring stage, from application to screening, interviews, offers, and final hire. This reduces administrative work and minimizes the risk of losing candidate data. Status updates and reminders ensure no applications are overlooked or delayed. The ATS streamlines the recruitment process and improves the overall candidate experience.

Resume Parsing & Candidate Database

The system scans and extracts relevant candidate information from resumes, including work history, skills, certifications, and contact details. Parsed data is stored in a searchable database, allowing recruiters to find suitable candidates quickly. This reduces the need for manual review and speeds up shortlisting. Historical candidate data can be used for future openings, improving hiring efficiency. Accurate data management ensures better decision-making and a more organized talent pool.

Interview Scheduling & Candidate Evaluation

Integrated scheduling tools allow recruiters to set interview slots, send automated invitations, and sync calendars with Google or Outlook. This prevents double bookings, scheduling errors, and confusion for candidates and interviewers. Digital evaluation forms let interviewers rate candidates on skills, cultural fit, and problem-solving ability. Evaluations are stored within each candidate’s profile, ensuring consistency, transparency, and fairness. Managers can easily compare feedback and make informed hiring decisions.

Collaboration Tools for Hiring Teams

Recruiters, HR managers, and department heads can share notes, feedback, and recommendations directly within the ERP. This centralizes communication and eliminates scattered email threads. All stakeholders remain aligned throughout the hiring process, improving decision-making speed. Collaboration ensures that candidates are assessed holistically and fairly. It promotes teamwork, transparency, and accountability across the recruitment cycle

Automated Onboarding Integration

Once a candidate accepts an offer, the ERP initiates onboarding tasks automatically, including employee record creation and assigning system credentials. Orientation sessions, access to training materials, and departmental introductions are scheduled seamlessly. This reduces manual administrative effort and ensures a smooth transition from candidate to employee. Employees are ready to contribute quickly, enhancing engagement and retention. Automation improves accuracy, compliance, and efficiency during the onboarding process.

Offer Letter Generation & Document Signing

The ERP automates the creation of offer letters with customizable templates covering salary, role, and benefits. Candidates can securely sign documents digitally, reducing paperwork and administrative delays. All documents are stored and managed within the system for future reference. This ensures a faster, more professional, and consistent candidate experience. HR teams can track offer acceptance and maintain compliance with employment policies
